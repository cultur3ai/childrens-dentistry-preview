After you send it

Three things happen, in this order.

  1. The form finds a time. It looks at our Monday–Thursday calendar for the days and time of day you chose, and holds the nearest opening for the kind of visit you picked.
  2. We call you. A person at the front desk confirms the day and time on the number you gave us. That call is the confirmation — not the screen you saw.
  3. You come in. Parents are welcome in the back for the whole visit, not just the waiting room. Bring your insurance card if you are using insurance, and photo ID.
