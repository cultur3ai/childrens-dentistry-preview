Before you come

Two things worth doing first.

  1. Request the appointment first. The form holds a time; the front desk calls to confirm it. Do not drive over on the strength of the screen alone.
  2. Bring the card and the ID. Insurance card if you are using insurance, and photo ID — it is faster at the desk than on the phone.

If your child is in pain or has swelling

Call (772) 283-1230 instead of using the form. The same goes for a knocked-out or broken tooth, bleeding that will not stop, or trouble breathing or swallowing. Those need a person on the phone today.

Bring them in. We’ll take it from the door.
