What we do

Three buckets, in the words we use with kids.

Preventing sugar bugs, treating sugar bugs, and — when a job is big — putting a child comfortably to sleep for it. Every visit happens in our own office on Coconut Ave.

01 · Preventing 02 · Treating 03 · Lights out

Services

01

Preventing sugar bugs.

The routine work, every six months, on a child who is not in pain yet. This is the part that keeps the rest of it small.

Exam
Every six months. The dentist looks at what is there, what is coming through, and how the brushing is going.
Digital tooth pictures
Digital x-rays, every six months as well. Digital means the pictures are on a screen in seconds, and there is no film to bite down on.
Cleaning
A cleaning with an assortment of toothpaste flavors — your child picks the one they want, which does more for a nervous visit than it sounds like it would.
Fluoride
Our tooth vitamins. A coating that makes the enamel harder for sugar bugs to get into.
Sealants
A thin coat over the grooves of the back teeth, where a toothbrush cannot quite reach, to keep the sugar bugs out of the places they like best.

02

Treating sugar bugs.

When one gets past the brushing. The work is the work — the part we take seriously is how the numbing feels and how long it lasts.

Tooth-colored fillings
The filling matches the tooth, so a repaired tooth still looks like their tooth.
Crowns
For a tooth that a filling will not hold. It covers what is left of the tooth and keeps it doing its job until the adult tooth arrives.
Extractions
When a tooth cannot be saved. We explain what comes next, because a missing baby tooth sometimes needs a plan of its own.
Space maintainers
A small appliance that holds the gap open after a tooth comes out early, so the teeth beside it do not drift into the space the adult tooth still needs.
Comfort: buffered lidocaine
We make the numbing as painless as it can be. Around here it is “sleepy juice” or “gel” — never “shot”.
Comfort: Oraverse
It reverses the numbness afterwards, cutting the numb time to about 45 minutes. A child can eat, drink and feel their lip again before the afternoon is over.

03

Lights out sugar bugs.

For the bigger jobs, or the child who needs more help than a kind voice can give that day. All of it happens here, in our own office.

Nitrous oxide
“Funny gas” — breathed through a small mask on the nose. It takes the edge off, it wears off quickly, and your child is back to themselves before you leave.
Oral sedation
Medicine taken by mouth before the visit, for a child who needs to be further away from the room than funny gas takes them.
IV sedation
Given here by a pediatric anesthesiologist from our partner, PDAA. It is a separate appointment from the treatment itself, and the consultation comes first.

Money

What things cost depends on what your plan covers.

We take all major PPO insurance plans and DentaQuest Healthy Kids, and CareCredit for anything spread over time. Families without insurance can use the yearly membership instead.

Insurance and the membership

No price list sits on this page, because the honest number depends on the tooth, the visit and the plan. Ask on the confirmation call and the front desk will go through what your plan covers for that visit.

Not sure whether something counts as a cleaning or a treatment visit? Say what is going on with your child and the front desk will tell you which appointment to book — or book it for you.

Not sure which visit to ask for?

Describe it and we will tell you. Pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth is a phone call, not a form — the rest can start here.
