What it changes

Two dentists, one practice.

Most practices have one dentist and a team. Here there are two, and they are mother and daughter. When a treatment plan is worth a second look, the second look is down the hallway rather than another appointment somewhere else.

It shapes the place in a quieter way, too. The practice has almost three decades behind it, and it has kept the things that make a child relax: the games, the tablets, the prizes and stickers, and a parent welcome in the back for the whole visit.
